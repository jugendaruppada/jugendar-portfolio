Who I am

A bit about me

I'm a first-year MBBS student at RIMS, Srikakulam. Alongside my studies, I run this portfolio as a personal project to help local businesses in Srikakulam get the social media presence they deserve.

Srikakulam is a third-tier city with no shortage of skilled, dedicated business owners — but most of them have little to no visibility online. I saw that gap and decided to do something about it.

My aim is to build this into a genuine portfolio business — visiting local shops and businesses across Srikakulam, understanding what makes each one unique, and turning that into content that gets them seen on social media.

Every business featured on this site is one I've personally visited and worked with. This page exists so anyone visiting the portfolio knows exactly who's behind it.
