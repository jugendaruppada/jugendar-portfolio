About

Small-city businesses deserve big attention

Srikakulam is full of businesses doing genuinely distinctive work — a family sweet shop with a forty-year-old recipe, a tailor who still cuts by hand, a hardware store that knows every customer by name. Most of them have no real presence online.

I visit, listen, and document. Every feature starts with time spent at the business — understanding what they make, how they make it, and why it matters to the people who already walk through their door.

From there, I build a short content piece — photos, a story, and a format suited to how people actually browse social media — and publish it as part of this growing portfolio.

Over time, this becomes a public directory of businesses in Srikakulam worth knowing about, and a body of work that shows what thoughtful local storytelling can look like.
